1965
Chronicle · Late Phase of the 1960s Bull Market

France Pulls Out Gold

S&P 500 +9.06% Dow Jones +10.9% Year-End 969 Main Theme Vietnam Escalation + Dollar-Gold Pressure + Rising Inflation
S&P 500 Nasdaq 100 & Dow Jones Chronicle

Overview

Great Society and Vietnam expand in parallel · France steadily converts dollars to gold · Year-end rate hike begins tightening

1965 opened with Johnson laying out the "Great Society" framework in his State of the Union — healthcare, education, environment, and anti-poverty programs bundled together, one of the most ambitious postwar fiscal expansions. The Dow rallied unilaterally through early February to 906. External pressure soon intensified: France announced on January 4 it would convert $35 million into gold, and on February 4 de Gaulle publicly proposed at the Élysée Palace a return to the gold standard, steadily moving gold out of the US at $35/oz — a direct challenge to Bretton Woods.

From February 11, Johnson escalated military intervention against North Vietnam; Operation Rolling Thunder was approved on March 2 for large-scale bombing; on March 8 Marines landed at Da Nang, formally drawing the US into ground combat in Vietnam. To ease dollar outflows, the US launched the Voluntary Foreign Credit Restraint Program (VFCR) as soft capital control. The index rallied from April to mid-May, then pulled back through June; Fed Chair Martin's June 1 Columbia speech warned of "permanent" inflation and balance-of-payments problems, while his June 25 Rutgers speech soothed markets.

In H2, fiscal expansion drove the economy into acceleration: the Social Security Amendments of 1965 were signed July 30, creating Medicare and Medicaid; the Voting Rights Act was signed August 6. The index rose from late June, with a sustained rally from September to an October peak. From November CPI rose sharply, and Johnson used administrative pressure to suppress aluminum and copper prices. On December 6 the Fed raised the discount rate from 4.0% to 4.5%, sparking a public clash between Johnson and Martin. The Dow closed at 969, up 10.9%; S&P 500 +9.06%.

Timeline of Events

General Context Significant Upward Catalyst Significant Downward Catalyst Key Historical Event (Events also marked on the chart below)
Jan 4
Johnson SOTU Lays Out the "Great Society" Agenda
A systematic package of healthcare, education aid, environmental protection, and anti-poverty programs — one of the most ambitious postwar fiscal expansion frameworks. The same day, France announced converting $35 million into gold within one month.
Feb 4
De Gaulle Élysée Palace Press Conference Proposes Return to Gold Standard
French Finance Minister d'Estaing simultaneously called for major financial powers to use gold in balance of payments. France converted dollars to gold at $35/oz, posing a direct challenge to Bretton Woods.
Feb 11
Johnson Orders Air Strikes on North Vietnamese Targets
Retaliation for North Vietnamese attacks on US facilities; US military intervention in Vietnam began to escalate.
Mar 2
Johnson Approves Operation Rolling Thunder
A sustained, large-scale strategic bombing campaign against North Vietnam, marking the full launch of direct US military involvement in the Vietnam War.
Mar 8
3,500 Marines Land at Da Nang
First regular US ground troops entered South Vietnam; the US was now fully engaged in the Vietnam War.
From Feb
Voluntary Foreign Credit Restraint Program (VFCR) Launched
The Johnson administration asked banks and corporations to voluntarily limit foreign capital outflows — a soft control to ease dollar pressure, signaling no immediate dramatic monetary tightening.
Jun 1
Fed Chair Martin Warns of Inflation at Columbia
Cited domestic inflation risk and balance-of-payments imbalances as potentially "permanent" problems, raising market concerns about sustained tightening.
Jun 25
Martin's Rutgers Speech Reassures Markets
Stated the US economy would not be denied the financing it needed for growth, easing tightening fears raised by the Columbia speech.
Jul 30
Social Security Amendments of 1965 Signed
Created Medicare for senior citizens and Medicaid for low-income families, delivering core legislation of the "Great Society."
Aug 6
Voting Rights Act of 1965 Signed
Removed legal barriers to Black voting in multiple states, implementing the 15th Amendment — landmark civil rights legislation.
Early Nov
White House Suppresses Aluminum Price Increase by Administrative Pressure
Reports disclosed presidential concern over aluminum prices, with administrative action blocking a 2% industry price hike; subsequent copper price rises raised similar concerns, reflecting emerging inflation pressure.
Dec 6
Fed Discount Rate: 4.0% → 4.5%
The big move to counter inflation and rapid fiscal expansion. Johnson and Martin publicly clashed over the timing of the hike, underscoring fiscal-monetary tension.

Overview

Brief banking crisis · AI capex narrative ignites · End of the hiking cycle

2023 opened with the chill of the 2022 bear still on the market, and broad consensus expected a recession. The debt ceiling was hit in January, the Fed continued to hike in February, and the macro was still tightening. The real turning point came in March—after crypto bank Silvergate's wind-down, Silicon Valley Bank (SVB) was closed on Mar 10, the second-largest US bank failure ever, followed by Signature Bank and First Republic. The Fed launched the BTFP (Bank Term Funding Program) as an emergency, and systemic-risk fears were quickly absorbed in mid-to-late March.

On May 30 Nvidia reported earnings and sharply raised guidance, surging 24% in a single day; the AI capex narrative formally launched, and Mag 7 and semiconductors became the year's strongest theme. The second half closed out the hiking cycle: Jul 26 was the final hike (5.25–5.5%); in August Fitch downgraded the US sovereign rating from AAA to AA+; in October the Israel-Hamas war broke out and long-bond yields briefly approached 5%; the index pulled back roughly 10% from August to October.

From November markets re-priced cuts; on Dec 13 Powell delivered a dovish FOMC pivot and the index completed a V-shaped reversal. The S&P 500 finished +24.23%, with the Nasdaq leading; bitcoin rose roughly 150% on the year as risk assets broadened together. The year set the two main themes of the next two years: the AI capex cycle and the rate-cut expectations game.

Timeline of Events

General background Materially pushed market higher Materially pushed market lower Key historical event (Events also mark the chart below)
Jan 19
US hits the debt ceiling
Treasury launches extraordinary measures to buy time, opening months of debt-ceiling brinkmanship.
Feb 1
Fed hikes 25 bp to 4.5–4.75%
The pace slows from 75 bp to 25 bp, but with sticky inflation the cycle is not yet over.
Feb 3
US shoots down Chinese "stray balloon"
US-China tensions spike; Secretary of State Blinken postpones his China trip.
Feb 24
Nvidia guidance well above expectations
Data-center business shows the first inflection signal for AI-accelerator demand.
Mar 8
Crypto bank Silvergate voluntarily winds down
The first domino of the banking liquidity crisis.
Mar 10
Silicon Valley Bank (SVB) is closed
The second-largest bank failure in US history. Signature Bank follows two days later; the regional banking sector comes under collective pressure.
Mar 12
Fed launches the BTFP (Bank Term Funding Program)
Allows banks to pledge Treasuries at par for one-year liquidity, containing the deposit run.
Mar 19
UBS acquires Credit Suisse for CHF 32 billion
A Swiss-government-orchestrated "shotgun" deal; AT1 bonds are written down in full, ending a century-old European bank.
Mar 22 / May 3
Fed continues hiking to 5.0–5.25%
Two more 25 bp hikes during the banking crisis—inflation takes priority.
Apr 27
First Republic Bank is closed
JPMorgan takes over; the second-largest US bank-failure record changes hands.
May 30
Nvidia beats earnings, up 24% in a single day
A sharply raised data-center outlook formally launches the AI capex narrative; Mag 7 and semis become the year's main theme.
Jun 3
Biden signs debt-ceiling deal, averting default
Suspends the debt ceiling through January 2025, ending months of political brinkmanship.
Jul 26
Fed hikes to 5.25–5.5%
The final hike of this cycle; the Fed then enters a holding pattern.
Aug 1
Fitch downgrades US sovereign rating from AAA to AA+
The second AAA strip by a major agency since the 2011 S&P downgrade; long-bond yields then accelerate higher.
Sep 27
Government shutdown scare
In the early hours of Oct 1 the House passes a short-term funding bill, briefly averting the shutdown.
Oct 7
Israel-Hamas war breaks out
Hamas launches a large-scale attack on Israel; geopolitical risk premia rise; crude and gold jump.
Nov 15
Xi-Biden meeting in San Francisco
Held during APEC; restores military-to-military communication and cooperation on fentanyl; US-China relations warm in stages.
Nov 29
FTX founder SBF found guilty on seven counts
The judicial coda to the 2022 crypto-industry collapse.
Dec 13
Fed signals rate cuts
The FOMC dot plot shows three cuts in 2024; Powell confirms hikes may be done. Markets shift into the "cut trade".
Dec 31
Bitcoin up roughly 150% on the year
Spot-ETF approval expectations resonate with the cut trade; risk assets broaden together.

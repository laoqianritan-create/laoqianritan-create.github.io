European and US Debt Crises Spread

Overview

Arab Spring · Japan's 3/11 quake · US loses AAA · Occupy Wall Street

2011 carried the late-2010 bull momentum into the new year with bullish sentiment. But in mid-February the Libyan conflict pushed oil higher, and on Mar 11 Japan's magnitude-9.0 earthquake and the Fukushima nuclear accident hit expectations in succession. From March, Moody's repeatedly downgraded Spain and the European debt crisis spread; in April the ECB hiked first, and S&P cut the US sovereign outlook to "negative". Between February and July the index oscillated in a wide range.

From late July to early August, US debt-ceiling talks deadlocked; both chambers finally signed a deal on Aug 2. But on Aug 5 S&P downgraded the US sovereign rating from AAA to AA+—the first downgrade in nearly a century. From Jul 25 to Aug 8 the S&P 500 fell 16.8% in a dozen sessions, with Treasury yields and crude crashing together. On Sep 20 the Fed launched $400 billion of Operation Twist, swapping short-dated paper for long-dated Treasuries to depress long-end yields. On Sep 17 Occupy Wall Street began in Zuccotti Park in New York and spread globally.

In late October an EU summit reached a €130 billion Greek rescue, briefly easing eurozone fears; US Q3 data improved and an oversold market rebounded. The S&P 500 closed flat at 1257 (0.00% YTD), with the Dow up 5.5%. Amid widespread losses in global equities, US stocks still stood out.

Timeline of Events

General background Materially pushed market higher Materially pushed market lower Key historical event (Events also mark the chart below)
Jan 14
Tunisian President Ben Ali ousted
The starting point of the Arab Spring; political upheaval subsequently spreads across MENA.
Feb 11
Egyptian President Mubarak steps down
After 30 years in power Mubarak resigns under mass protests; the Arab Spring spreads.
Mar 11
Magnitude-9.0 Tohoku earthquake + Fukushima nuclear accident
Triggers a tsunami and a radioactive release at the Fukushima Daiichi plant, reshaping global supply chains and the nuclear-power industry.
Mar
Libyan war breaks out
The US, France, and UK launch air strikes on Libya. Crude jumps from $85 through $100/bbl.
May 1
US forces kill Osama bin Laden in Pakistan
Navy SEALs execute Operation Neptune Spear—a closing chapter to nearly a decade of post-9/11 counter-terror operations.
May
IMF chief Strauss-Kahn arrested in NY scandal
Dominique Strauss-Kahn is arrested in New York and resigns in June, disrupting eurozone-bailout talks.
Jul
US debt-ceiling negotiations deadlock
Both parties square off intensely as the Aug 2 statutory default date approaches. Obama signs a deal on Aug 3.
Aug 5
S&P downgrades the US sovereign rating from AAA to AA+
Outlook negative—the first downgrade in nearly a century, triggering a sharp global risk-off response.
Aug
Apple overtakes ExxonMobil in market cap for the first time
Apple becomes the world's most valuable listed company—an emblematic handoff from the energy era to the tech era.
Sep
Fed launches Operation Twist
Swaps $400 billion of short-dated debt for long-term Treasuries to depress long-end yields and support the economy.
Sep 17
Occupy Wall Street begins
Starts in Zuccotti Park in New York, protesting financial-industry greed and inequality; spreads globally.
Oct
EU summit reaches Greek rescue agreement
The 14th summit agrees to extend a €130 billion Greek assistance program, temporarily easing eurozone debt fears.
Oct 20
Libyan leader Qaddafi killed
After 42 years in power, Qaddafi is killed by opposition forces in Sirte, ending the Libyan war.
Dec 18
Last US troops withdraw from Iraq
The nearly nine-year Iraq War formally ends as combat units complete their withdrawal.

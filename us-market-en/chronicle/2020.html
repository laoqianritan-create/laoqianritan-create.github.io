COVID — Fastest Bear and Fastest Rebound

S&P 500 +16.26% Max Drawdown -34% Mar 23 Low 2237 Main Theme Pandemic + ZIRP + Unlimited QE + Fiscal Relay
S&P 500 Nasdaq 100 & Dow Jones Chronicle

Overview

Down 34% in 22 sessions · ZIRP + unlimited QE · Full recovery by August

2020 was split in half by a global pandemic. On Feb 19 the S&P 500 was still making record highs; as COVID spread through Europe and the US, US equities tripped circuit breakers four times in ten days (Mar 9, 12, 16, 18); on Mar 23 the index bottomed at 2237, down 34% from its high—a bear in just 22 sessions, the fastest on record.

The policy response was equally unprecedented. The Fed cut 50 bp on Mar 3, cut another 100 bp to zero on Mar 15, and launched $700 billion of unlimited QE; on Mar 23 it announced corporate-bond ETF purchases. On the fiscal side, the $2.2 trillion CARES Act passed on Mar 27, with another $900 billion in December. A liquidity deluge plus a stay-at-home economy let the Nasdaq and large-cap tech decouple first; the S&P 500 had recouped all its losses by Aug 18—the fastest bear-to-bull switch ever.

The second-half narrative shifted to the election, vaccines, and retail traders. The May Floyd killing ignited the BLM movement; on Nov 3 Biden was elected; on Nov 9 Pfizer reported 90% Phase-3 vaccine efficacy, lifting value and cyclicals. On Apr 20 WTI futures went negative at -$37; in December the Reddit-driven GameStop short-squeeze began to brew, foreshadowing 2021. The S&P 500 finished +16.26%; the NBER-dated February–April recession was also the shortest on record.

Timeline of Events

General background Materially pushed market higher Materially pushed market lower Key historical event (Events also mark the chart below)
Jan 3
US strike kills Iranian general Soleimani in Baghdad
US-Iran tensions briefly approach open conflict; crude jumps short-term.
Jan 21
First US COVID case confirmed (Seattle)
Traced to a traveler from Wuhan; the CDC starts initial contact tracing.
Jan 31
Trump announces China travel ban
Non-citizens who had been to China within 14 days are barred from entry; global aviation starts to shut down.
Feb 5
Senate acquits Trump in impeachment trial
Neither article clears the 67-vote threshold; Trump is acquitted.
Mar 3
Fed delivers emergency 50 bp cut
An inter-meeting move—the first since the 2008 crisis—taking fed funds to 1.00%–1.25%.
Mar 9 / 12 / 16 / 18
US equities trip circuit breakers four times in 10 sessions
The mechanism had been triggered only once (in 1997) since being introduced in 1988; now four times in two weeks. The Saudi-Russia oil war compounds a global asset selloff and dollar shortage.
Mar 15
Fed cuts another 100 bp to zero and launches $700 bn of unlimited QE
Fed funds returns to 0–0.25%; $700 billion of asset purchases is announced and later made open-ended.
Mar 23
S&P 500 bottoms at 2237—the fastest bear in history
Down 34% in 22 sessions from the Feb 19 high. The Fed announces investment-grade corporate-bond ETF purchases the same day, extending the rescue into credit markets.
Mar 27
$2.2 trillion CARES Act signed
The largest one-shot relief package in US history—direct checks, PPP small-business loans, and expanded unemployment insurance.
Apr 20
WTI May futures fall to -$37
Cushing storage runs out and longs are forced out—the first negative print in oil futures history.
May 25
Floyd killing ignites nationwide BLM protests
A Minneapolis police killing of a Black man triggers protests in hundreds of US cities through the summer.
Jun 8
NBER dates the 2020 recession: February → April
Just two months, the shortest recession on record—ending the longest expansion since June 2009.
Aug 18
S&P 500 reclaims pre-pandemic high—fastest bear-to-bull on record
Less than five months from the Mar 23 low; large-cap tech leads.
Nov 3
Biden defeats Trump in 2020 election
Democrats take the White House; Senate control hinges on the January 2021 Georgia runoffs.
Nov 9
Pfizer reports 90% Phase-3 vaccine efficacy
The first large-sample Phase-3 readout for an mRNA vaccine; value, airlines/hotels, and small caps rally in unison.
Dec 27
$900 billion second relief bill signed
Includes $600 stimulus checks and extended UI; averts a government shutdown.
Dec
GameStop retail short-squeeze begins to brew
Reddit's WallStreetBets coalesces around GME's high short interest, setting up the January 2021 squeeze.

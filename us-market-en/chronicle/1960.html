Overview

Fundamentals slip back into recession · U-2 incident derails Four-Power Summit · October London gold price reveals the "First Dollar Crisis"

The 1958–1959 rally ended abruptly in early 1960, with the Dow falling from 680 to 599 by early March, a 12% decline. NBER later determined that the US economy re-entered recession from April 1960, with industrial production YoY growth declining sharply through the year.

On May 1, a U-2 reconnaissance plane was shot down over Soviet airspace; the planned Paris Four-Power Summit collapsed on May 17, US-Soviet tensions resurged, and defense stocks became a catalyst in a weak market. On June 2 the Fed cut the discount rate from 4.0% to 3.5%, with the Dow rebounding to a peak of 656. From late July through early August, policy support intensified: margin requirement cut from 90% to 70%, discount rate cut again to 3.0%, and two reserve requirement cuts during the year.

OPEC was founded in Baghdad on September 14. On October 21, the London gold price spiked to $41.5/oz, 18.57% above the official rate, marking the first crisis of confidence in the dollar's reserve status under Bretton Woods — the "First Dollar Crisis." Triffin's "Triffin Dilemma" was published the same year in Gold and the Dollar Crisis. Kennedy narrowly won the presidency on November 8, sparking a "Kennedy rally." The Dow closed at 616, down 9.3%; S&P 500 -2.97%.

Timeline of Events

General Context Significant Upward Catalyst Significant Downward Catalyst Key Historical Event (Events also marked on the chart below)
Apr
NBER Identifies Recession Onset
Later definition: the US economy re-entered recession in April 1960. Industrial production YoY growth slid rapidly through 1960.
May 1
U-2 Reconnaissance Plane Shot Down Over Soviet Airspace
Pilot Gary Powers was captured. On May 5 Khrushchev made the announcement public, and US-Soviet relations again deteriorated.
May 17
Paris Four-Power Summit Collapses
The US president refused to apologize for the U-2 incident; Khrushchev left early. Defense stocks subsequently became a catalyst in the weak market.
May
First MIDAS Early-Warning Satellite Launched
Developed in response to Soviet ballistic missile systems, the space-based infrared early-warning system added to defense-stock buying.
Jun 2
Fed Discount Rate: 4.0% → 3.5%
First rate cut of the year.
Jul 27
Stock Margin Requirement: 90% → 70%
Fed lowered the margin requirement.
Aug 11
Fed Discount Rate: 3.5% → 3.0%
Second rate cut of the year.
Aug 28
OAS Adopts the San José Declaration
Invoked the Monroe Doctrine again to intervene in Cuban affairs. On September 2 the Havana million-person rally adopted the Havana Declaration condemning US intervention.
Sep 1
Fed Cuts Reserve Requirement: 18.0% → 17.5%
Continued monetary easing.
Sep 14
OPEC Founded in Baghdad
Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, and Venezuela formed a production and pricing alliance to break Western oil-majors' dominance.
Oct 21
London Gold Price Spikes to $41.5/oz
18.57% above the official rate — the dollar's first crisis of confidence as reserve currency under Bretton Woods, the "First Dollar Crisis." The same year, Yale's Robert Triffin published the "Triffin Dilemma" in Gold and the Dollar Crisis.
Nov 8
Kennedy Narrowly Defeats Nixon for the Presidency
Markets repriced for Democratic policy expectations — the "Kennedy rally."
Dec 1
Fed Cuts Reserve Requirement Again to 16.5%
Second cut of the year, leaving monetary space for the incoming administration.

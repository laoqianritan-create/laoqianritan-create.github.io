2015
Chronicle · A Breather in the Decade-Long Slow Bull

First Fed Hike in Nearly a Decade

Overview

Tug-of-war over hike timing · 8/11 RMB reform jolts the world · Fed delivers its first hike in 9 years in December

2015's storyline was a repeated tug-of-war between "hike expectations" and "weak data". The Fed had ended QE in October 2014 and markets expected the first hike since 2006—this "Sword of Damocles" hung overhead all year. Oil kept falling early in the year and Q4 2014 GDP slowed; the index rallied nearly 6% in February on better eurozone PMI and a Greek aid extension. From March to mid-August it ranged at the highs, with the Nasdaq strongest on tech earnings.

On Aug 11 the RMB reform sent the currency down more than 3% in a flash; combined with weakening Chinese PMI, it ignited fears of global demand. On Aug 24 "Black Monday" saw thousands of A-share stocks hit the daily limit-down and global stocks plunged; the Dow opened down 1,000 points. From Aug 17 to Aug 25, a mere seven sessions, the S&P 500 fell 11%—its sharpest weekly pullback since October 2008. On Sep 17 the Fed unexpectedly held.

On Dec 16 the Fed hiked 25 bp, taking the fed funds range to 0.25–0.50%—the first hike in nearly a decade. All three indices closed higher on the day. But weak earnings, low oil, and a strong dollar combined to leave the S&P 500 down 0.73% on the year, its first annual decline since 2008.

ECB QE plus Fed-hike expectations drive the DXY to a 12-year high, weighing on US multinationals' overseas revenue and EM assets.

Greece defaults on the IMF · 7/5 referendum rejects austerity
Greece becomes the first developed country to default to the IMF; the Jul 5 referendum rejects austerity 61%; on Jul 13 the Eurogroup agrees on a third bailout.
Jun 26
Supreme Court legalizes same-sex marriage nationwide
Obergefell v. Hodges is decided 5–4; all 50 states recognize same-sex marriage—a societal milestone.
Jul 14
Iran nuclear deal (JCPOA) signed
Iran and P5+1 reach a comprehensive deal in exchange for sanctions relief; expectations of Iran's oil return weigh further on prices.
Aug 11
RMB 8/11 reform with a one-off depreciation
The PBOC adjusts the central-parity mechanism; the RMB falls nearly 2% in a day against the dollar and over 3% in a few days, jolting global asset pricing.
Aug 24
Global Black Monday · Dow opens down 1,000 points
Thousands of A-shares hit limit-down, Europe and Asia drop in tandem; the Dow is down over 6% intraday, triggering circuit-breaker-style volatility in US stocks.
Sep 17
Fed unexpectedly holds rates in September
The FOMC keeps rates near zero amid EM turbulence and global demand concerns; the market's hike timeline is pushed back.
Sep
Volkswagen diesel-emissions scandal exposed
EPA charges Volkswagen with installing defeat devices in 11 million diesels; about a third of the company's market cap evaporates in days.
Nov 13
Paris terrorist attacks
Coordinated ISIS attacks kill 130; European debates on counter-terrorism and refugees intensify.
Dec 16
Fed hikes 25 bp—first in 9 years
Fed funds target moves from 0–0.25% to 0.25–0.50%, ending the zero-rate era. The dot plot signals four more hikes in 2016.
Dec 18
WTI crude falls to $35/bbl
OPEC's no-cut stance, expected Iranian return, and a strong dollar push crude to its lowest since the 2009 financial crisis.
